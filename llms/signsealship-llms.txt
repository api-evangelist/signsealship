# SignSealShip

> SignSealShip (signsealship.com) lets anyone e-sign a document, get it notarized by a live commissioned notary via remote online notarization (RON), and arrange tracked physical delivery — a prepaid self-ship label or fully automated print-and-mail — all in one online flow. Pay per use, no membership. Customers bring their own completed documents.

Key facts:
- Services: e-signature (from $12), remote online notarization (from $49, first seal and identity verification included), physical delivery (automated print-and-mail of completed-document copies via mailing partners at a flat $12.95, or carrier shipping at a $14.95 handling fee plus live-rated postage) — available individually or combined.
- Pricing model: itemized. The notary fee is always passed through at cost and never inflated by document type; platform fees (workflow, rush, handling) are separate disclosed line items. Postage and government fees are never marked up or discounted.
- Add-ons: rush handling $19.95, extra seal $15, extra signer $15, witness on demand $19, overnight handling $14.95. (Prices as published at https://signsealship.com/pricing; every quote is itemized before payment.)
- Notarizations are performed by independent, state-commissioned notaries or approved RON provider partners, available 24/7. Identity verification = knowledge-based questions (KBA) + government-ID credential analysis. Most sessions take about 15 minutes.
- Remote online notarization is fully authorized in 43 U.S. jurisdictions — 42 states plus Washington, D.C. Signers in most non-RON states (e.g., California, Georgia, Mississippi, Alabama, North Carolina, South Dakota) can use an out-of-state online notary, with exceptions (Georgia excludes real-property and vehicle-title documents; Connecticut excludes real estate). South Carolina does not currently recognize RON. Wills and certain estate documents are excluded in several states (e.g., NJ, NY, WI, LA).
- E-signatures are legally binding under the U.S. ESIGN Act and UETA. SignSealShip runs the signing ceremony on BoldSign and adds its own evidence layer: every executed document receives a cryptographic tamper-evident seal (visible in Adobe Acrobat's signature panel), a SHA-256 hash-linked audit trail, ESIGN §101(c) consumer-consent records, and a court-grade certificate of completion.
- Business pricing and optional partner plans — Partner Link ($49/mo, 5% off workflow fees), Pro Office ($149/mo, 10%), Closing Desk ($399/mo, 15% + priority notary routing, dedicated closing coordinator, and client analytics dashboard included); client analytics dashboard is a +$99/mo add-on on Partner Link/Pro Office — exist for title companies, attorneys, real estate agents, lenders, and property managers. Clients of business users never need accounts or subscriptions.
- SignSealShip is a technology platform: it does not provide legal advice, choose legal documents, or draft or review documents.

## State pages

Cross-state reference (free, CC BY 4.0): https://signsealship.com/ron-laws compares all 51 U.S. jurisdictions on effective date, notary fee cap, identity verification, record retention, signer location and document exclusions, with the statute cited for every value. Per-requirement comparisons at /ron-laws/effective-dates, /ron-laws/notary-fees, /ron-laws/identity-verification, /ron-laws/record-retention, /ron-laws/signer-location and /ron-laws/document-exclusions. Machine-readable at /ron-laws.csv and /ron-laws.json. Archived at Zenodo with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22213684, which is the citation to use. Values are reported as the cited sources state them and are not legal advice.
